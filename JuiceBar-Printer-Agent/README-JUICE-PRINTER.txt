================================================================
  SULTAN RESTAURANT - JUICE BAR DEDICATED PRINTER SETUP
================================================================

1. Copy this folder ("JuiceBar-Printer-Agent") onto the Juice Bar PC
   (or download directly from http://192.168.1.10/juicebar/kds -> "Connect Juice Printer").

2. Make sure the Juice Bar USB Thermal Printer is plugged into the Juice Bar PC.

3. Double-click "Test-JuiceBar-Printer.bat" to confirm the Juice Bar printer prints a test slip.

4. Double-click "Install-AutoStart-On-Boot.bat" ONCE:
   - Starts the Juice Bar Print Agent immediately (minimized).
   - Automatically starts every morning when the Juice Bar PC turns on!

Zero conflict guarantee:
- Kitchen orders print ONLY on the Kitchen printer (POS-80 on 192.168.1.10).
- Juice Bar orders (Hot Coffee & Tea, Ice Coffee, Mocktail, Ice Cream, Cold Drinks & Water)
  print ONLY on the Juice Bar USB printer!
